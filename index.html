2. Essential Academic Rules

Multiple Section Policy

Allocation of the same course for multiple sections is strictly prohibited for any faculty member.

Exception: If a faculty is allotted three theory courses, the same course may be allotted for two sections. However, the third course must be a distinct subject.

HoD Relaxation

2 hours of relaxation in TCH is permitted for HoDs to facilitate departmental administrative duties.

Professor Mandate

Senior Professors and Professors must compulsorily handle at least one Practical course or Project work.

3. How to Use the Portal

1 Authentication: Log in via your HITS Institutional Email. Access is only permitted during the active collection window.
2 Preference Selection: Assign values (1-5) to subjects.
* Note: If another faculty claims a specific preference number for a subject, you must choose a different number (2-5).
3 Auto-Saving: The system implements a slight delay to ensure database consistency. Wait for the "Saved" indicator before closing.
4 Final Submission: Once you reach 5 preferences, click Review & Final Submit. This action is permanent and locks your selections.
